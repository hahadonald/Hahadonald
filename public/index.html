Exhibit 001Sept 2019

The Sharpie Amendment

"My favorite part isn't the map — it's that an entire federal agency had to publicly disagree with a Sharpie."— curator's take

Read the file

During a briefing on Hurricane Dorian, a National Weather Service map appeared to have been altered with a black marker to extend the storm's projected path into Alabama — a state the official forecast hadn't included. Nobody in the room ever quite explained who held the pen. The internet gave it a name within the hour: Sharpiegate.

Source: NOAA, AP, and most major outlets, September 2019.

Exhibit 002Apr 2020

The Light Bulb Idea

"The kind of live TV moment where you can watch several staffers age in real time."— curator's take

Read the file

At a White House coronavirus briefing, the idea of getting disinfectant or UV light "inside the body" to fight the virus was floated out loud as something worth looking into. Poison control hotlines reported a jump in calls the next day. The idea was walked back almost immediately — but not before becoming a permanent case study in what not to say from a podium during a pandemic.
